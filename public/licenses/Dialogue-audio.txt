MIKA DUO: original game dialogue, release 1.4.

The two included MP3 files are locally synthesized performances of the game's
Russian dialogue. They do not require a speech service or a system voice.
Synthesis: Piper, ru_RU-dmitri-medium; pitch, timbre and level adjusted for the
fictional character. No voice recordings or neural model weights are included.
Voice model card and source-data attribution:
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/ru/ru_RU/dmitri/medium
https://github.com/OHF-Voice/voice-datasets
The model card identifies its training dataset as CC0.
