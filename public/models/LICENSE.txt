Modifications for this application include a layered rose hairstyle, recolored
eyes and skin shading, tailored open vest, fitted cotton wrap, pleated skirt,
waist sash, sculpted leg warmers, hairpins, optimized geometry and textures.
The new model geometry and textures are also released under CC0-1.0.

The screenshot and portrait are renders of the bundled 3D model, not substitutes
for interactive geometry. No reference artwork is embedded in the model.
